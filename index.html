Fast refreshers

Quick notes to remember

Short explanations of key ideas. Use these as a starting point, then check your class notes and exam-board specification.

🧬

Cells and energy

Mitochondria are the main site of aerobic respiration. Ribosomes make proteins. Plant cells also have a cellulose cell wall and may contain chloroplasts for photosynthesis.

⚗️

Atoms and bonding

Atoms contain protons and neutrons in the nucleus, with electrons around it. Ionic bonding is attraction between oppositely charged ions; covalent bonding involves shared electron pairs.

⚡

Electricity

Current is the rate of flow of charge. Potential difference is energy transferred per unit charge. Ohm’s law can be written as V = I × R when conditions stay constant.

📐

Algebra essentials

Collect like terms, use inverse operations to solve equations, and expand brackets by multiplying every term inside. Check by substituting your answer back in.

📊

Ratio and proportion

Simplify a ratio by dividing every part by the same common factor. In direct proportion, the ratio between the quantities remains constant.

📖

English analysis

Make a clear point, select a short quotation, identify a language or structural choice, and explain its effect. Link your interpretation to the question.
